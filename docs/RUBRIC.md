# Suggested grading rubric

A starting point for grading the financial model assignment (100 points). Edit freely. The same rubric appears on the **Rubric** sheet of every Excel export, with blank Score and Comments columns, and each criterion names where to look on the **Instructor Summary** sheet.

| Criterion | Points | Strong work | Developing work | Where to look |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business model logic | 15 | The chosen model and drivers match how the venture actually earns money. Revenue traces to concrete operational activity (customers, units, hours, deals). | Model choice is unclear or drivers are generic; revenue depends on a growth rate with no operational story. | Summary header; Assumptions (revenue drivers) |
| Assumption quality and justification | 25 | Example values replaced or explicitly confirmed. Every major assumption has a specific basis; key drivers are evidence- or benchmark-based, and speculative ones are acknowledged. | Many example values remain, bases are missing or vague (“estimate”), confidence is unrated. | Assumption support counts; Assumptions and their basis |
| Costs and team plan | 15 | Costs are complete and not double counted. Hiring is timed to need, founders’ pay is addressed, direct and operating costs are separated sensibly. | Missing cost categories, unpaid founders not discussed, hires far ahead of revenue without explanation. | Assumptions (team, operating expenses) |
| Cash flow and funding | 15 | Payment timing is realistic for the customer type. The student identifies the cash low point and how much funding is needed and when. | Assumes customers pay immediately without reason; treats positive ending cash as viability despite an earlier shortfall. | Results by scenario; cash chart |
| Scenarios and sensitivity | 15 | Downside and upside change specific, justified drivers and describe real situations. The student knows which assumptions matter most. | Scenarios left at the suggested ±20% values with no explanation. | Results by scenario; most influential assumptions |
| Critical review and reflection | 15 | Thoughtful responses to the review prompts. The reflection states what must be true for the venture to work and how each will be tested. | Prompts unanswered or answered defensively; no plan to test key assumptions. | Review prompts and responses; reflection |
| **Total** | **100** | | | |

## Notes for grading

- The tool deliberately does not judge whether a venture is good. Grade the reasoning, not the outcome: a model that honestly shows a large funding gap can earn full marks.
- Values marked “Example” came with the template. Students may keep one if they confirm it (by rating its confidence) and give a basis.
- The confidence scale is Evidence-based (direct evidence about this venture), Benchmark-based (published data or comparable companies) and Speculative (a best estimate).
- Students can see this rubric and the summary page themselves, which is intended.
